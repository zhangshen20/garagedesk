Voice reference sources (used only as zero-shot conditioning audio for Chatterbox TTS)

OWNER voice reference: refs/owner_gwd.wav (19.6 s, 24 kHz mono)
  Source:   LibriVox "Dialect and Accent Collection Vol. 1", track 15,
            "The North Wind and the Sun" (attributed to Aesop),
            read by Graham Dunlop (LibriVox reader "GWD") in an Australian accent.
  URL:      https://archive.org/details/dialect_accent_0909_librivox
  File:     https://archive.org/download/dialect_accent_0909_librivox/dialectaccent_vol_01_15gwd.mp3
  Licence:  Public domain (LibriVox; archive.org licenseurl http://creativecommons.org/licenses/publicdomain/;
            the recording itself states "This recording is in the public domain").
  Excerpt:  13.2 s - 32.8 s of the track (story text only, no LibriVox intro), loudness-normalised.

Also tried (not used in final): refs/owner_tf.wav, same collection, track 24, read by Timothy Ferguson
  ("cultivated Australian accent"), public domain, 14.4 s - 31.5 s. One take of each owner line was
  generated with it (in raw_tf/, final_tf/); not used because a cultivated accent suits a mechanic less.

Checked but not usable: EdAcc (edinburghcstr/edacc, CC BY-SA 4.0) - no Australian-accent speakers
  in either the validation or test split.

NARRATOR voice: Chatterbox's built-in default voice (conds.pt shipped with ResembleAI/chatterbox, MIT).

TTS model: Resemble AI Chatterbox (chatterbox-tts 0.1.7, ResembleAI/chatterbox on Hugging Face), MIT licence.
  Outputs carry Resemble's imperceptible Perth watermark (built into the library).
ASR used for listen-checking: OpenAI Whisper large-v3-turbo (MIT) via mlx-whisper.
